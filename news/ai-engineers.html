AI Engineers

As Indian businesses increasingly adopt AI, the role of AI engineers is becoming crucial in delivering business value. Forward deployed engineers, on the other hand, have limited scope. Indian companies must understand the difference between these two roles to make informed decisions.

Introduction to AI Engineers and Forward Deployed Engineers

In the Indian market, AI is becoming a key driver of business growth. As companies adopt AI, the demand for skilled professionals is on the rise. Two roles that are often discussed in this context are AI engineers and forward deployed engineers. While both roles are important, they have different responsibilities and career potential.

Role of AI Engineers

AI engineers are responsible for developing and implementing AI solutions that can drive business value. They have a broad range of skills, including machine learning, data science, and software engineering. In the Indian context, AI engineers can help companies like those in the BFSI sector, which is regulated by the RBI, to develop AI-powered solutions that can improve customer experience and reduce costs.

Role of Forward Deployed Engineers

Forward deployed engineers, on the other hand, are typically deployed on-site with customers to implement and support specific solutions. They have a more limited scope than AI engineers and are often focused on a particular product or technology. While they play a critical role in ensuring customer satisfaction, their career potential may be limited compared to AI engineers.

Comparison of AI Engineers and Forward Deployed Engineers

In terms of career potential, AI engineers have a broader range of opportunities. They can work on a variety of projects, from developing chatbots for customer service to creating predictive models for business forecasting. Forward deployed engineers, while important, have a more limited scope and may not have the same level of career advancement opportunities.

  • Broader range of skills required
  • Greater career potential
  • More opportunities for innovation
  • Higher demand in the Indian job market
  • Greater business value potential
