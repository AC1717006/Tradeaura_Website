Android Dev Update

Google's new policy requires Android app developers to register with verified identity, affecting the Indian mobile development market. This change impacts all Android apps, including those installed outside the Play Store. Indian developers must adapt to this new requirement.

Android App Registration Mandatory

Starting September 2026, Google is introducing a new policy for Android app developers. Every developer will be required to register centrally with a verified identity before their app can be installed on any certified Android device. This policy applies to all Android apps, including those installed from the Play Store and sideloaded APKs.

In the Indian context, this policy change may have significant implications for mobile app developers. Many Indian businesses and startups rely on Android apps to reach their customers, and this new requirement may add an extra layer of complexity to the development process.

Key Implications

The new policy has several key implications for Indian developers:

  • Verified identity registration is mandatory for all Android app developers
  • All apps, including sideloaded APKs, are subject to this policy
  • Developers must register centrally before their app can be installed on certified Android devices
  • This policy change may impact the Indian mobile development market and ecosystem
  • Indian developers must adapt to this new requirement to ensure their apps remain accessible to users
